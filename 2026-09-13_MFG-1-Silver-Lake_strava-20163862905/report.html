How this was measured
  • Race start = launch from a standstill.
  • Gate = where your final effort ends (2 s before the end of the race window).
  • Timing gate at 47.89192, -122.21468. Positions sit a median 1.7 m from the lap centreline. With 1 Hz GPS, read lap times as good to about 1 s and sector times to about 1 s each; differences smaller than that are noise.
  • Course distance comes from matching each GPS point to the median line of all laps, with the odometer keeping the match from jumping between neighbouring lanes. Sector boundaries are placed at fast points, where timing is most precise.
  • The recording has 8 s with no data (auto-pause or dropouts); those seconds are counted as elapsed time.
