How this was measured
  • Race start = launch from a standstill.
  • The end of the race is not on a repeated part of the course; using the fastest clean straight instead.
  • Gate = fastest clean straight (not necessarily the finish line).
  • Heart rate looks like a strap dropout for 12 s of the race (it falls to double digits while power stays up); those seconds are left out of HR averages.
  • Timing gate at 47.66766, -122.34674. Positions sit a median 1.6 m from the lap centreline. With 1 Hz GPS, read lap times as good to about 1 s and sector times to about 1 s each; differences smaller than that are noise.
  • Course distance comes from matching each GPS point to the median line of all laps, with the odometer keeping the match from jumping between neighbouring lanes. Sector boundaries are placed at fast points, where timing is most precise.
  • The recording has 228 s with no data (auto-pause or dropouts); those seconds are counted as elapsed time.
